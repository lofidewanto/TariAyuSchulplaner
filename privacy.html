Privacy policy – TariAyu School Planner

As of October 2026

In short

TariAyu School Planner collects no data about you. No ads, no analytics, no account.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter – timetable, homework, grades, notes and photos – stays on your device. The app sends nothing to the internet, not to us either. We cannot see your entries.

Backups and sharing

Only you create a backup, and you decide where it goes. When you share your plan, you choose what to share and with whom.

Permissions

The app asks for notifications, calendar and Face ID only when you use the feature. It only adds to your calendar and never reads your events. Face ID is checked by iOS – the app never sees your face.

Deleting

In the app, Datenschutz → „Alle Daten löschen“ removes everything at once. Deleting the app removes your data too.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we store no data about you, we hold nothing to disclose – your entries are only with you.
